## Full description

deessa Foundation, in collaboration with the Kathmandu University School of Education (KUSOED), cordially invites you to participate in the Nepal Accessibility Summit 2026.

Centered around the theme, "Accessible Nepal 2026: Advancing Inclusive Education, Health, Social Protection, and Urban Futures," the summit will bring together policymakers, educators, researchers, development partners, disability advocates, practitioners, and community leaders to exchange knowledge, share best practices, and foster innovative solutions for building a more accessible and inclusive Nepal.

The two-day summit will feature keynote addresses, panel discussions, technical sessions, workshops, and networking opportunities, providing a platform for meaningful dialogue and collaboration on accessibility across education, healthcare, social protection, and urban development.
