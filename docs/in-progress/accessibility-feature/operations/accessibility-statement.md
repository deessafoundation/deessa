## Our Commitment to Accessibility

deessa Foundation is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to ensure we provide equal access to all of our users.

---

## Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

**The deessa Foundation website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.**

- **Partially conformant** means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard.
- Our goal is to achieve and maintain full conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

### Standards We Follow

- **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** - Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (primary standard)
- **Section 508** - U.S. federal accessibility standards
- **EN 301 549** - European accessibility standard (harmonized with WCAG)

## Accessibility Features

We have implemented numerous features to make our website accessible to the widest possible audience:

### Built-in Accessibility

**Keyboard Navigation:**
- All functionality is available using only a keyboard
- Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements
- Skip links to bypass repetitive content
- Logical tab order throughout the site

**Screen Reader Support:**
- Semantic HTML5 markup
- Proper heading hierarchy (H1-H6)
- Alternative text for informative images
- ARIA labels and live regions where appropriate
- Form labels and error messages
- State changes announced to assistive technology

**Visual Design:**
- Sufficient color contrast (4.5:1 minimum for normal text)
- Text remains readable when scaled to 200%
- Content reflows appropriately on mobile devices
- No information conveyed by color alone
- Resizable text without loss of functionality

**Motion and Animation:**
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` system setting
- No auto-playing audio
- Videos can be paused, stopped, and controlled
- Manual controls for carousels and slideshows

### Customizable Accessibility Settings

We provide a built-in accessibility panel (blue button, bottom-right corner) with these features:

1. **Text Size:** Adjust from 100% to 200%
2. **Line Spacing:** Customize spacing between lines (1.5-2.5)
3. **Letter Spacing:** Add space between letters (0-12%)
4. **Font Family:** Choose Default, System, or OpenDyslexic font
5. **High Contrast:** Enhanced contrast for better readability
6. **Reduce Motion:** Disable animations and automatic movement
7. **Sensory-Friendly Mode:** Simplified visual design with reduced stimulation

[Learn more about our accessibility features](/accessibility-guide) _(link to user guide)_

## Known Limitations

We strive for full accessibility, but acknowledge the following known limitations:

### In Progress

**Third-Party Content:**
- Some embedded content from third parties (social media, videos, payment providers) may not fully meet accessibility standards
- We are working with vendors to address these issues
- Alternative access methods are provided where possible

**PDF Documents:**
- Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible
- We are in the process of remediating existing documents
- New documents are created with accessibility in mind
- Request alternative formats: [accessibility@deeshafoundation.org]

**Media Alternatives:**
- Some videos may not yet have captions or audio descriptions
- We are working to add captions to all video content
- Transcripts are provided for podcasts
- Contact us if you need specific content in an alternative format

### Planned Improvements

- Enhanced focus indicators beyond browser defaults (Q1 2027)
- Live region announcements for preference changes (Q1 2027)
- Additional language support for accessibility panel (Q2 2027)
- Comprehensive video captioning (Ongoing)

## Privacy and Data

### Accessibility Preference Storage

When you customize accessibility settings:

- **Storage Location:** Your browser (localStorage), not our servers
- **Data Collected:** Only your preference settings (text size, contrast, etc.)
- **Sharing:** Never transmitted to our servers or third parties
- **Privacy:** Not linked to your identity or tracked
- **Control:** You can clear settings anytime via browser settings

**Legal Basis:** These are "strictly necessary cookies" under GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and are exempt from consent requirements.
