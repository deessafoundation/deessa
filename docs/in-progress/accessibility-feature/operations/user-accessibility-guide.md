# Accessibility Features Guide

**Welcome!** This guide explains how to customize your browsing experience on the Deesha Foundation website to meet your individual needs.

---

## Quick Start

Look for the blue **Accessibility** button in the bottom-right corner of any page:

```
[Blue circular button with accessibility icon]
Bottom right corner of the screen
```

Click or tap this button to open the accessibility settings panel.

---

## Available Features

### 1. Text Size

**What it does:** Makes all text on the website larger or smaller.

**How to use:**
- Click the **-** button to make text smaller
- Click the **+** button to make text larger
- Range: 100% (normal) to 200% (double size)

**Best for:**
- People with low vision
- Anyone who finds the default text too small or too large
- Reading on high-resolution screens

**Tips:**
- Start at 125% and adjust from there
- The site layout automatically adapts to larger text
- Works great with browser zoom too!

---

### 2. Line Spacing

**What it does:** Increases or decreases the space between lines of text.

**How to use:**
- Drag the slider from "Compact" to "Spacious"
- Or use arrow keys (← →) when the slider is focused
- Range: 1.5 to 2.5 line height

**Best for:**
- People with dyslexia
- Anyone who finds dense text hard to follow
- Improving reading comprehension

**Tips:**
- Try 2.0 for comfortable reading
- Combines well with larger text size
- Default setting uses the site's original spacing

---

### 3. Letter Spacing

**What it does:** Adds extra space between individual letters.

**How to use:**
- Drag the slider to increase spacing
- Range: 0% (normal) to 12% (extra wide)

**Best for:**
- People with dyslexia
- Visual tracking difficulties
- Reducing visual crowding

**Tips:**
- Start with 4-6% for subtle improvement
- Higher values (10-12%) for significant spacing
- Some users prefer this with dyslexia-friendly fonts

---

### 4. Font Family

**What it does:** Changes the font used across the entire website.

**How to use:**
- Select from the dropdown menu:
  - **Default:** Our standard website font
  - **System:** Your device's native font (faster loading)
  - **OpenDyslexic:** Specially designed for readers with dyslexia

**Best for:**
- People with dyslexia (OpenDyslexic font)
- Faster loading on slow connections (System font)
- Personal font preference

**About OpenDyslexic:**
- Free, open-source font designed by a dyslexic designer
- Letters have weighted bottoms to prevent flipping/swapping
- Unique letter shapes reduce confusion
- May not work for everyone - try it to see!

**Tips:**
- System font uses your device's default (like San Francisco on Mac)
- OpenDyslexic takes a moment to download first time
- Works with all text size and spacing settings

---

### 5. High Contrast

**What it does:** Increases the contrast between text and background colors for better readability.

**How to use:**
- Click the "High Contrast" button to turn **ON** or **OFF**
- When ON, you'll see pure black text on pure white background

**Best for:**
- People with low vision
- Color blindness or color perception difficulties
- Bright sunlight or poor lighting conditions
- Eye strain or sensitivity

**What changes:**
- Text becomes pure black (#000000)
- Backgrounds become pure white (#ffffff)
- Borders and outlines become more prominent
- Links are clearly underlined
- Buttons have stronger borders

**Tips:**
- Pairs well with larger text size
- Your device's built-in contrast settings will also be respected
- Works on all pages and in dark rooms

---

### 6. Reduce Motion

**What it does:** Stops automatic animations and movements on the website.

**How to use:**
- Click the "Reduce Motion" button to turn **ON** or **OFF**
- When ON, all automatic animations stop immediately

**Best for:**
- People with vestibular disorders or motion sensitivity
- Seizure prevention (photosensitivity)
- Concentration and focus
- Migraine prevention
- Reducing distractions

**What stops when enabled:**
- ✅ Hero carousel autoplay
- ✅ Testimonial carousel autoplay
- ✅ Automatic video playback
- ✅ Ken Burns effects (zooming images)
- ✅ Scroll-triggered animations
- ✅ Fade-in and slide-in effects
- ✅ Loading spinners (become static)

**What still works:**
- ✅ You can manually click carousel arrows
- ✅ You can click to play videos
- ✅ Page transitions are instant (not removed)
- ✅ Hover effects (simplified)

**Tips:**
- Your browser's motion settings are automatically respected
- Manual controls always work - you're in charge!
- If enabled, videos won't replay automatically after you pause them

---

### 7. Sensory-Friendly Mode

**What it does:** Simplifies the visual design by removing decorative elements that can be distracting or overwhelming.

**How to use:**
- Click the "Sensory-Friendly Mode" button to turn **ON** or **OFF**
- Creates a calmer, less stimulating visual experience

**Best for:**
- People with autism or sensory processing sensitivity
- ADHD or attention difficulties
- Anxiety or stress reduction
- Reducing visual overwhelm
- Focusing on content over decoration

**What changes:**
- ✅ Shadows removed from cards and buttons
- ✅ Image saturation reduced (less vibrant colors)
- ✅ Decorative patterns simplified or hidden
- ✅ Gradient backgrounds may be simplified
- ✅ Visual "noise" minimized
- ✅ Motion also disabled (same as Reduce Motion)

**What stays the same:**
- ✅ All content remains visible and accessible
- ✅ Important images still show
- ✅ Text hierarchy and layout preserved
- ✅ Functional elements unchanged (buttons, forms, etc.)

**Tips:**
- Try combining with high contrast for maximum clarity
- Great for long reading sessions
- Reduces eye strain for many users
- Creates a "quiet" visual experience

## Resetting Settings

### Reset Individual Settings

Each setting has a small reset icon (↻) next to it. This only appears when you've changed that setting from its default.

**To reset one setting:**
1. Find the reset icon next to the setting name
2. Click the icon
3. That setting returns to default (others stay as you set them)

### Reset All Settings

**To reset everything at once:**
1. Scroll to the bottom of the accessibility panel
2. Click the "Reset All" button
3. All settings return to their default values

**Note:** Resetting does not delete your preferences - you can always adjust them again anytime!

---

## Your Settings Are Saved

### Automatic Saving

✅ **Your preferences are automatically saved** to your browser whenever you make a change.

✅ **They persist across pages** - once set, your preferences apply to every page you visit on our website.

✅ **They persist across sessions** - close your browser, come back tomorrow, and your settings will still be there.

### Where Settings Are Stored

Your accessibility settings are stored **locally on your device** using your browser's localStorage:

- 🔒 **Private:** Only stored on your device, never sent to our servers
- 🔒 **Local:** No other websites can access your settings
- 🔒 **Secure:** Not linked to your identity or tracked in any way

### Clearing Your Settings

**To clear your accessibility settings:**

**Method 1: Use Reset All**
- Open the accessibility panel
- Click "Reset All" button

**Method 2: Clear Browser Storage**
- Your browser's "Clear browsing data" settings
- Select "Cookies and site data" for deeshafoundation.org

### Private/Incognito Browsing

When using private or incognito mode:
- ✅ Settings work during your session
- ⚠️ Settings are cleared when you close the browser (this is normal for private mode)
- 💡 Tip: Use regular browsing mode to keep your settings

## Keyboard Navigation

All accessibility features are fully keyboard accessible!

### Opening the Panel

1. Press **Tab** key until the blue accessibility button is focused
2. Press **Enter** or **Spacebar** to open

### Navigating Controls

- **Tab:** Move to next control
- **Shift + Tab:** Move to previous control
- **Arrow Keys:** Adjust sliders (line spacing, letter spacing)
- **Spacebar/Enter:** Toggle buttons (High Contrast, Reduce Motion, etc.)
- **Escape:** Close the accessibility panel

### Screen Readers

All controls have proper labels and state announcements for screen readers like:
- NVDA (Windows)
- JAWS (Windows)
- VoiceOver (Mac/iPhone)
- TalkBack (Android)

## Tips and Recommendations

### Getting Started

**First time user?** Try these combinations:

**For comfortable reading:**
- Text Size: 125-150%
- Line Spacing: 2.0
- Letter Spacing: 4-6%

**For dyslexia:**
- Font Family: OpenDyslexic
- Text Size: 125%
- Line Spacing: 2.0-2.2
- Letter Spacing: 6-8%

**For low vision:**
- Text Size: 150-200%
- High Contrast: ON
- Line Spacing: 2.0

**For motion sensitivity:**
- Reduce Motion: ON
- (This will automatically stop all animations)

**For sensory sensitivity:**
- Sensory-Friendly Mode: ON
- (This also enables Reduce Motion)
- High Contrast: Consider trying

### Combining Settings

Most settings work great together! Popular combinations:

- 🌟 **Comfortable Reading:** Text Size 125% + Line Spacing 2.0
- 🌟 **Maximum Clarity:** High Contrast + Text Size 150% + Line Spacing 2.0
- 🌟 **Calm Experience:** Sensory-Friendly + High Contrast
- 🌟 **Dyslexia Support:** OpenDyslexic Font + Spacing increases

### What If Something Looks Wrong?

If a page looks broken or text is cut off:

1. **Try reducing text size** to 150% or lower
2. **Check browser zoom** - try resetting to 100% (Ctrl/Cmd + 0)
3. **Try a different browser** - Chrome, Firefox, Safari all work
4. **Report it to us** - see Contact below

Most layouts adapt beautifully up to 200% text, but some complex pages may need attention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to use accessibility features?

**No!** Accessibility features work for everyone, whether you're logged in or not. No account required.

### Will my settings work on my phone and computer?

Settings are saved per-device and per-browser. If you use:
- Chrome on your laptop and Safari on your phone, you'll need to set preferences on each
- The same browser on the same device, settings persist

### Do accessibility settings affect how others see the site?

**No.** Your settings are personal to your browser only. They don't change the website for anyone else.

### Can I use these features with a screen reader?

**Yes!** All features are fully compatible with screen readers like NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and TalkBack.

### What if my browser's storage is full?

If localStorage is full, we automatically fall back to sessionStorage (works during your visit) or memory-only mode. The features still work, but settings may not persist after you close the browser.

### Are my accessibility preferences tracked or shared?

**No.** Your settings are stored only on your device, never sent to our servers, and never tracked or analyzed. Complete privacy!

### Can I suggest new accessibility features?

**Absolutely!** We're always looking to improve. Contact us at [accessibility@deeshafoundation.org]

### Will future updates affect my settings?

We may add new features over time, but your existing settings will be preserved. If we need to change how settings work, we'll migrate your preferences automatically.

## Technical Details

*For those interested in how it works:*

### Storage

- **Key:** `deesha-a11y-preferences`
- **Location:** Browser localStorage (persistent)
- **Fallback:** sessionStorage (session-only) if localStorage unavailable
- **Size:** ~150 bytes (well within limits)
- **Format:** JSON

### Privacy

- **Local-only:** Never leaves your device
- **No tracking:** Not sent to analytics
- **GDPR compliant:** Strictly necessary cookie exception
- **User control:** Delete anytime via browser settings

### Performance

- **Bundle size:** ~13 KB JavaScript (gzipped)
- **CSS impact:** ~4 KB (gzipped)
- **Load time:** No impact on First Contentful Paint
- **Font loading:** OpenDyslexic loads only when selected (~35 KB)
